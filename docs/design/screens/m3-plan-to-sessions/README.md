## Rules the screens carry

- **One course per line**, created from the line (filled in from it) or linked from the library; a line whose needs named a course is linked from the start. Lines may share a course. Left-out lines get none. Editing the course later never changes the approved plan.
- **Create sessions only after the course.** The proposal suggests **one session per month of the line's quarter** (Q1 = A): 3 sessions → Jan / Feb / Mar; 2 → the first and the last month. Seats follow the plan's split (16 / 16 / 15) and the plan's session count is kept (Q2 = A).
- **Branch:** filled in when all the line's people are in one branch; when they are in several, the people are placed by branch and L&D checks each session's branch (Riyadh 32 → two sessions of 16, Jeddah 15 → one). A virtual session may have no branch (*Online*).
- **Planned participants** are the people named in the needs; departments that gave «names later» keep their seats and are shown as «Sales 15 · names later». Nobody is invited or enrolled (that is WP-LD-08 / WP-LD-09).
- **A draft holds** its line, course, month, branch, seats, planned participants and estimated cost (from the plan's cost per session). **No day, time, room or trainer** yet.
- **Only the L&D team sees drafts** (L&D Manager, Training Coordinator); every change is audited; a draft can be deleted freely.
- **A draft may move outside its quarter** (Q3 = A); it then carries «outside the planned quarter» on its card and in the plan line.
- **Plan revised after drafts exist:** drafts stay; the line shows «3 drafts, plan says 2» and L&D deletes one. Nothing is deleted automatically.
- Months follow the organization's date display (Gregorian here; Hijri organizations see Hijri months, as in the real screenshots).
- Required fields carry a **red asterisk**. Popups follow Kawadir: 672 px for a rich create / edit, wider (860 px) only for the multi-row sessions popup. «Cancel» comes before the primary button; a destructive action sits at the start of the footer.

## States

| Thing | States shown |
|---|---|
| Plan line — Course | *none* («Create course» / «Link») · *linked* (name, code, how it came) · *left out* (nothing needed) |
| Plan line — Draft sessions | *After the course* (0 of n) · *Create sessions* (0 of n) · *n draft sessions* (months) · *3 drafts, plan says 2* (not drawn: amber text, same cell) |
| Draft session | *Draft* (dashed card) · *outside the planned quarter* (not drawn: an amber mark on the card) · *Scheduled* (solid card, next step WP-LD-08, only in the legend) |
| Month / quarter | sessions and seats · «No sessions» · «Not drafted yet in Qn: …» with the next action |
| Empty calendar (not drawn) | «No sessions yet. Sessions come from the approved training plan.» with **Open the plan** (or, with no approved plan, «Approve a training plan first»). |

## Phone behaviour

- The calendar opens in **List** (months with cards); the Year grid is offered from 768 px up.
- Quarter filter as pills; branch and course behind one **Filters** button.
- The plan table becomes **one card per line** (as M3 screen 1p) with the Course and Draft sessions actions as buttons at the bottom of each card.
- Popups become **full-screen sheets**; the sessions popup shows **one card per session** (month, branch, seats, people) instead of rows; buttons sit in a bar at the bottom.

## Example used in every screen (fake names)

Training plan 2027, approved · v1: 12 lines (10 in the plan, 2 left out), 313 people, 16 sessions, SAR 131,900 (budget SAR 135,000). **Advanced Excel**: Finance 20 + Sales 15 + Human Resources 12 = 47 people, 8–20 per session, SAR 9,000 per session → 3 sessions (16 / 16 / 15), SAR 27,000. Branches: Finance, Human Resources and IT in Riyadh, Sales in Jeddah, Operations in Dammam. At the moment of screens 1, 4 and 5: 7 of 10 lines have a course, 5 have their drafts, 11 of 16 sessions drafted (248 seats, SAR 68,000). Screens 2 and 3 show Advanced Excel earlier in the same week (no course, then course without sessions). Every figure is computed by `build.js`, so all screens agree.

## How the mock-ups are made

`build.js` (with `base.js`, the helpers and token CSS of the M3 mock-ups) writes static HTML (`html/`) with the Jadarat token values and renders the PNGs with Playwright at 1440 px (phone 390 px), scale 2. It is design material only, not product code.

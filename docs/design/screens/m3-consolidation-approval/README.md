## Rules the screens carry

- **Lines are built for L&D, and every line starts in the plan** (Q1, Q2 = A). Needs for the same course or the same topic become one line. Lines can be merged (bulk bar or the «look alike» hint), and a need can be moved out of a line (⋯ in the line popup). Leaving a line out **needs a reason**.
- **Classes and cost are worked out**: people ÷ maximum per class, keeping the minimum (47 people, 8–20 → 3 classes of 16/16/15). Cost = classes × cost per class. A **shared line's cost is split by people** («share of the cost»). A line without a cost per class shows *Needs a cost* and is not counted; it must be costed before submitting.
- **When = quarter** (Q3 = A; PO 14 Nov 2026). It is preselected from the quarter most departments asked for. The month is chosen at scheduling (WP-LD-04).
- **Budget warns, never blocks.** Within shows green with what is left. Over shows **amber when less than 10 % over and red at 10 % or more**, and needs **one-line reason** before submitting. Approvers see the reason next to the figures.
- **Submit freezes the plan.** The **HR Manager role** approves: any holder can, the first decision counts, and nobody approves a plan they submitted. **Finance confirms only when Planning settings → «Finance confirms the plan» is on** (off by default; the submit popup shows the step as *off*).
- **Send back = one comment for the whole plan** (Q5 = A). The plan returns to L&D as a draft with the comment shown at the top, and is resubmitted through the same steps.
- **Approved = version 1, locked.** «Revise plan» opens version 2, approved the same way, and every version is kept. **Department Heads learn the result, including left-out lines with reasons, only when the plan is approved** (Q4 = A).
- Required fields carry a **red asterisk**. Popups follow Kawadir: 448 px for one question, 560 px for a confirmation with a summary, 672 px for a rich edit. «Cancel» comes before the primary button, and optional fields sit behind «More».

## Phone behaviour

- Tables become **one card per row** (screen 1p). The same applies to the budget (one card per department, with the meter and the reason field) and to the review page.
- Groups are folded except the first one. «Group by» is a pill menu, and the bulk bar stays above the cards.
- Popups become **full-screen sheets**, with the buttons in a bar at the bottom.
- The main action (*Submit for approval*, or *Approve* / *Send back* for the HR Manager) is in a bar fixed at the bottom.

## Example used in every screen (fake names)

Five departments (Sales, Human Resources, Finance, Operations, IT) with budgets of SAR 35,000 / 15,000 / 20,000 / 30,000 / 22,000 (SAR 122,000 in all). There are 13 lines when the Plan tab first opens and 12 after one merge. The plan has 313 people, 16 classes and SAR 119,900. Sales is over by SAR 4,473 (red), Finance by SAR 181 (amber) and IT by SAR 4,407 (red). The organization total is within budget, with SAR 2,100 left. Excel – advanced formulas: Sales 20 + Human Resources 15 + Finance 12 = 47 people, 8–20 per class at SAR 5,000 → 3 classes, SAR 15,000. Every figure is computed by `build.js`, so all screens agree.

## How the mock-ups are made

`build.js` writes static HTML (`html/`) with the Jadarat token values and renders the PNGs with Playwright at 1440 px (phone 390 px), scale 2. It is design material only, not product code.
